Who We Are

Warren's Shop-at-Home Flooring Experts

Warren Carpet and Floors specializes in residential and commercial carpet and flooring — and we bring our store right to your door. With over 20 years of experience and an excellent reputation in the Warren community, we offer almost every type of flooring you could want: carpet, hardwood floors, laminate, ceramic tile, custom vinyl, and more. Because we don't carry the huge overhead of a retail store, we pass those savings directly on to you.

What We Offer

Flooring for Every Room & Every Budget

From budget-friendly carpet for basements to high-end hardwood for living rooms — we have the right product, the right price, and the expertise to back it up.

Carpeting

Thousands of carpet styles, brands, and colors — Berber, loop, plush, level cut, and more. We have carpet for every budget, from economical basement options to premium bedroom and stair carpet. Shaw Carpet, Kraus, and many more.

Hardwood Floors

Decades of hardwood flooring experience. We help you select the right wood based on traffic patterns, sunlight, location, and budget. Expert installation and refinishing for homes throughout Warren MI.

Laminate Floors

Laminate has become one of the most popular flooring choices thanks to stunning new colors and styles at a price well below hardwood. Durable, beautiful, and easy to maintain — perfect for busy households.

Ceramic Tile & Vinyl

Ceramic tile, custom vinyl tile, and luxury vinyl plank for kitchens, bathrooms, and beyond. We carry a wide range of styles from traditional to modern and work with both residential and commercial customers.

Commercial Flooring

From lobbies and conference rooms to entire apartment complexes — we handle commercial flooring projects of all sizes. We work quickly to minimize disruption to your business and provide free estimates for all commercial jobs.

Fast Installation

Need it done quickly? We offer 48-hour installation on in-stock products. For other orders, we can typically schedule installation within days of your initial call. Evening and weekend appointments available.
