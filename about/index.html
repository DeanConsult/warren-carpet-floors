Our Story

Warren's Trusted Flooring Company

Warren Carpet and Floors has been serving the Warren MI community and Metro Detroit area for over 20 years. We started with a simple idea: bring the flooring store to the customer instead of making them drive all over town.

That shop-at-home model has made all the difference. Our customers love being able to see samples right in their own space, matched against their actual furniture and wall colors. And because we don't carry the overhead of a retail showroom, we can pass those savings directly on to you.

We have almost every type of flooring you could want — carpet, hardwood, laminate, ceramic tile, custom vinyl, and more. We work with residential and commercial customers and have a great reputation in the Warren community built on fair pricing, honest advice, and quality installation.
